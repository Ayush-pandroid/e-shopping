Project Statement

Project Title

Product Store – E-Commerce Shopping Application

1.PROBLEM STATEMENT

Online shopping requires users to browse products, search for items, compare prices, manage their shopping cart, and place orders efficiently. A simple and user-friendly shopping application is needed to demonstrate these basic e-commerce operations in one platform.

The Product Store project aims to provide a desktop-based e-commerce shopping experience where users can browse products from different categories, search and filter products, add items to a cart, view the total price, and complete purchases. The application also maintains previous order records so users can review their order history and total spending.

2. SCOPE OF THE PROJECT

The project focuses on developing a basic e-commerce shopping application for desktop users. It covers the complete basic shopping process, from product discovery to checkout and order-history management.

The scope includes:

- Displaying products from multiple categories.
- Searching for products by name or category.
- Filtering products according to category and price.
- Managing products and their available stock.
- Adding and removing products from the shopping cart.
- Calculating product subtotals and the total order price.
- Completing the checkout process.
- Saving completed orders for future reference.
- Displaying previous orders and total spending.
- Providing product images with fallback icons when images are unavailable.

The project is designed as a basic e-commerce prototype and does not currently cover real online payment processing, user accounts, or real-world delivery management.

3. TARGET USERS

The application is intended for:

- Online Shoppers – Users who want to browse products, add items to a cart, and place orders.
- Students and Beginners – Users who want to understand the basic working of an e-commerce application.
- Small Store Demonstrations – Businesses or developers who want a simple prototype for demonstrating product browsing and ordering.
- Project Evaluators – Users who need to test basic e-commerce functionalities such as searching, filtering, cart management, checkout, and order history.

4. HIGH-LEVEL FEATURES

Product Catalog

- Displays products across multiple categories.
- Shows product name, price, category, and stock availability.
- Supports multiple product types such as Electronics, Footwear, Apparel, Home & Kitchen, Fitness & Sports, and more.

Search and Filtering

- Search products by name or category.
- Filter products by category.
- Filter products using a maximum-price slider.
- Reset filters to return to the complete catalog.

Shopping Cart

- Add products to the cart.
- Increase or decrease product quantities.
- Remove products from the cart.
- Clear the entire cart.
- Automatically calculate subtotals and the total cart price.

Stock Management

- Displays current product availability.
- Prevents users from adding products when stock is unavailable.
- Shows low-stock warnings.
- Updates stock after a successful checkout.

Checkout

- Displays the total purchase amount before placing an order.
- Allows the user to confirm the purchase.
- Records completed orders with date, products, quantities, and total price.

Order History

- Saves previous orders in a JSON file.
- Displays previous orders through a dedicated interface.
- Shows order date, purchased items, and order total.
- Calculates and displays the total amount spent on previous orders.

Product Images

- Supports local product images.
- Can download missing product images when an internet connection is available.
- Uses emoji icons as a fallback when images cannot be loaded.

PROJECT GOAL

The overall goal of the project is to create a simple, functional, and user-friendly e-commerce shopping application that demonstrates the fundamental operations involved in an online shopping system, while providing a practical example of Python-based application development.
